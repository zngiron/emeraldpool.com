### Choose from up to 16 unique JetPak® massages
JetPaks are revolutionary modular jetted spa seats unique to the Bullfrog Spas brand. Each JetPak creates a unique hydromassage experience by targeting specific areas of your back, neck, and/or shoulders, while varying in massage type and intensity. With so many unique and compelling hydrotherapy experiences available, you can completely personalize your spa by selecting your favorite JetPaks.
Choose from up to 16 different JetPak options to create the perfect hydromassage experience for your body, your your lifestyle, and your home.

### Place your favorite JetPak in your favorite seat
No matter your height, body type, or preferred seat style, Bullfrog Spas® has a comfortable seat that works just for you.
Relax in a premium lounger, enjoy a full-body massage in a deep seat, or hang out in a shallow seat to cool down or avoid wet hair. With varying seat types and depths, everyone gets an ideal experience, and all can enjoy the spa together.
The JetPak Therapy System makes your spa experience that much better. Simply place your favorite JetPak massage in your favorite seat and relax.

### Adjust your massage to your preferred intensity
What’s your ideal massage intensity?
You may not have the same preference in hydromassages as your friends or loved ones, and your preference may change from time to time based on your activities. Use the JetPak® therapy dial to turn the intensity up after a tough exercise or a stressful day of work, or choose a gentle pressure when escaping or relaxing with friends or family.
To adjust your massage for that “just right” feel, simply dial in your perfect massage intensity.

### Frequently asked questions about the JetPak Therapy System
- Are JetPaks available to purchase individually? You can purchase additional JetPaks from us. We can order you the correct color and shape JetPak for your spa.
- How do I clean the JetPaks? For optimum spa care, each time the spa water is changed, remove all JetPaks. Clean the back manifold area on with a spa surface cleaner and a long, soft bristle brush. For cleaning the JetPak acrylic surface, use a mild dishwashing soap, window cleaner, or other products recommended by your local authorized Bullfrog Spas Dealer. For stubborn stains, use a mild acrylic cleaner or a mild detergent. To apply these cleaners, use a soft, damp cloth or sponge. Rinse well and dry with a clean cloth. Special Care For R Series Injection Molded JetPaks: High levels of sanitizer and normal use over time can have a bleaching effect on the injection molded (dark gray) Jetpaks. These can be cleaned / buffed to look like new again with the aid of automotive polishing compound found at any automotive retailer. Simply use a soft towel to rub this compound onto the plastic. Then buff with a dry towel.
- How do I interchange the JetPaks? Step 1: Turn off all jet pumps. You may also wish to put the spa in “Standby.” This will prevent the pump(s) from activating. Step 2: Remove the JetPak cap to access the JetPak. For M Series spas the cap remains attached to the spa. To access M Series JetPaks use both hands to lift the edge of the cap nearest the center of the spa to release the snap. Gently rotate the cap up and then slide back to access the JetPak.
- For most other series (R Series, 2022 and earlier Swim Series, 2022 and earlier A Series, and STIL) remove the head rest and Snap-Cap entirely by lifting upwards.
- For 2023 and newer A Series and Swim Series models the cap stays attached to the JetPak and this step is not necessary.
Step 3: While applying pressure downward on the black JetPak manifold push (or pull from the inside) the manifold toward the inside of the spa to release the manifold from the wall clip assembly, then lift the JetPak straight up to remove.
Caution! While performing steps 3 and 4, you must use the black manifold area as the point of contact. Pressure applied to the front face (acrylic plate) may cause damage to the JetPak and void the warranty.
Step 4: Exchange JetPak.
Step 5: Lower the manifold of the new JetPak carefully as to align the lower valve assembly and gasket into the bulkhead fitting. Apply pressure downward as you move the JetPak manifold until the manifold is secured in the wall clip assembly.
Step 6: Replace the Snap-Cap and head rest over the JetPak and press down firmly to snap into place. For M Series snap caps press down on the front part of the Snap-Cap to place and then press firmly on the rear portion of the Snap-Cap to reengage the snap feature and secure in place.
- How do I know which JetPaks to select? Many dealers have test spas on-premise where you can try the different JetPak® massages to get a feel for which ones you like best. Contact your dealer to see if that option is available to you.
- Use the images and videos on our JetPak Selection Page to understand which area of the back, neck, or shoulders the JetPak massage jets are targeting. Select the JetPaks that target areas of your body that frequently need pain relief, or select a JetPak with a variety of jet types for ultimate relaxation.
- Use our online Design Studio tool. First, select your spa model and colors, You’ll then have the option of selecting and placing JetPaks. You can select the “Let Us Suggest JetPaks” button and we will place the recommended JetPaks for that spa model into the spa. These recommended JetPaks have been carefully selected to provide a variety of jet types and intensities.
- How do I change the massage intensity on a JetPak? To adjust the water flow to JetPak jets turn the valve located in the lower portion of each JetPak. To increase jet water pressure, turn the valve handle counterclockwise.
- To decrease jet water pressure, turn the valve handle clockwise.
NOTE: To allow for proper circulation, the valve located in M Series®, A Series®, R Series® & STIL® JetPaks is designed to adjust jet pressure, but will not completely stop jet flow.
