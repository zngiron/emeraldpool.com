### About Us
Meet The Emerald Pool & Patio Family

### About Emerald Pool - Did You Know?
Emerald Pool & Patio was established in 1955 by Grandpa Neely as a pool construction company that focused solely on building inground gunite pools. His previous experience in the concrete industry allowed him to offer the community reliable pool design and construction at a time when pools were still considered specialty items.

### Why Choose Emerald?
Throughout the years, Emerald Pool & Patio has continued to expand into many aspects of the outdoor living segment, including above-ground pools , portable hot tubs , grills , pool and spa supplies , parts and services , and much more. Now operated by third-generation owner Vance Neely, Emerald Pool & Patio continues to provide the same quality customer service that made them a household name.
When you choose to work with Emerald Pool & Patio, you’re choosing a company that has years of history behind it. Our core foundation is built on quality and integrity, and we look forward to providing you with nothing less than a wonderful experience every time you visit.
Stop by either one of our retail locations in Eugene or Bend to see all of the products we carry for your complete backyard oasis. We also have complete parts , service and maintenance departments for your pool , spa , fireplace and stove , and a chemical lab that offers free delivery and price matching.
Contact Emerald Pool & Patio today to take care of all your outdoor living needs and to become a part of a family that strives to provide the community with nothing less than 100% customer satisfaction while building backyards you can enjoy for years to come.

### The Emerald Pool & Patio Team
At Emerald Pool and Patio, we’re dedicated to providing our clients with a pool-building experience like no other, and that starts with having professionals you can trust to get the job done. The Emerald Pool and Patio is comprised of a number of experienced and qualified professionals who are dedicated to providing clients with an unforgettable pool design, building, and maintenance experience, and we understand the importance of providing a quality product and quality service that is unmatched throughout the area. We take pride in bettering ourselves through industry involvement in an effort to best serve you, and we look forward to closely working with you.
