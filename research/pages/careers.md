### A Great Place to Work
Emerald Pool and Patio is looking for a new member to join its team. Emerald Pool and Patio is a family-owned business serving the community of Oregon since 1955. From the beautiful outdoor pools at Camp Harlow and the Eugene Country Club to the elegant indoor swimming pools at Sheldon and the Dixon Aquatic Center, we strive for excellence in every aspect of our business. EPP continues to be a leader in the pool, patio, and spa industry year after year. Respect, kindness, excellence, honesty, integrity, and professionalism are the pillars we lean on for every decision we make in our day-to-day operations. These shared values and attitudes help breed the culture and character we continuously strive for. EPP offers on-the-job training and numerous opportunities for advancement. We are looking forward to hearing from you soon.
All of our positions are full-time and offer on-the-job training and an opportunity for growth. Company health insurance, vision, dental, and 401k are available.
