### Emerald Pool & Patio
At Emerald Pool & Patio, we specialize in outdoor living. We have been providing Eugene and Bend, Oregon with quality pools , jacuzzis , spas , hot tubs , stoves , patio furniture , outdoor kitchens and barbecues , and much more since 1955.
Are you looking to warm up your winter or enjoy a casual outdoor living lifestyle while entertaining family and friends?
Do you just want to get away from it all with a relaxing spa or hot tub? You’ll find the right combination of outdoor living solutions you’re looking for at Emerald Pool & Patio.

### Professional Staff, Expert Builders
Emerald Pool & Patio was established in 1955 by Grandpa Neely as a pool construction company that focused solely on building inground gunite pools. His previous experience in the concrete industry allowed him to offer the community reliable pool design and construction at a time when pools were still considered specialty items.
At Emerald Pool and Patio, we’re dedicated to providing our clients with a pool-building experience like no other, and that starts with having professionals you can trust to get the job done.

### Exceptional Service
Our friendly service crew does a variety of repair, installation, and other maintenance needs for your swimming pools, spas, hot tubs, and other related items. Whether it is indoor or outdoor equipment we sell or items you have purchased elsewhere, we can service it.
We also have the largest pool and hot tub chemicals, supplies, and parts department in the state of Oregon. We carry several brands for regular hot tub and pool maintenance. Our Pool and Spa experts will help you choose the right products to keep your water sparkling and clean all season long.

### We Customize Your Paradise
At Emerald Pool and Patio, we want you to have the backyard oasis you’ve always dreamed about, and that’s why we design and construct custom inground pools and spas. Made with Gunite technology, our custom pools and spas are made to be durable and robust. Simply, they are built to last!
We specialize in the highest quality commercial and private custom-built pools and spas. A list of our satisfied clients includes Kah Nee Tah Warm Springs , Eugene Country Club , Oakway Fitness , as well as many city pools, such as Echo Hollow , Sheldon , and River Road Park . Emerald Swimming Pools of Oregon, Inc. has been a selective member of the Master Pool Guild for over 40 years.
