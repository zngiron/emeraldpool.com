Which series is which

Series is the decision that narrows everything else. Read these and you will already know which two models to sit in.

A Series

The luxury line. Every seat backs onto a JetPak you can lift out and swap, so the massage follows the person rather than the shell.

M Series

Elite construction: more water, more pumps, elevated seating and the hidden water returns. The M9 is the biggest spa we stock.

X Series

The same shell and insulation without the JetPak wall. The most spa per pound, and the easiest to get through a side gate.

STIL

A flush square cabinet and a low profile. The one that looks least like a hot tub when the cover is on.

Calm

One pump, seven seats, the same cover and insulation as everything above it. The line we recommend when the brief is simply "a hot tub".

Swim Series

Swim at one end, soak at the other. Three lengths, from twelve to seventeen feet.
