This site is built to WCAG 2.2 AA. If any part of it stops you getting what you came for, tell us and we will fix it — and answer your question by phone in the meantime.

What we have done

  • Every text and background pairing on the site is checked against WCAG 2.2 AA — 4.5:1 for body copy, 3:1 for large text and for controls.
  • Every interactive element has a visible focus ring, and the whole site can be operated from the keyboard alone.
  • There are no pop-ups, no overlays, no cookie wall and no newsletter interrupt. Nothing on this site covers what you were reading.
  • Nothing animates if your device asks for reduced motion, and no video plays on its own.
  • Photographs carry alternative text; product drawings carry the model they describe.
