1955 — a man with a concrete mixer

Started with concrete

Emerald Pool & Patio was founded in 1955 by Grandpa Neely, who came out of the concrete trade and started building inground gunite pools at a time when a pool was still an unusual thing to want. The business grew the way a good trade business does: someone's neighbour saw the work, and called.

Seventy years on it is run by third-generation owner Vance Neely, and it has widened into the rest of the back yard — above-ground pools, hot tubs and swim spas, hearth, grills, patio furniture, parts and chemicals. What did not change is the part that is hard to buy: a parts counter, a service department and a chemical lab staffed by people who will tell you that your cover is the problem rather than sell you a new spa.

Twelve of us are technicians and delivery crew. None of them are subcontracted, and most have been here longer than the spas they go back out to fix.

Four reasons, none of them a discount
