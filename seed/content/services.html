What the vans carry

What happens when you book

  • You tell us what it is doing. Model, age and symptom through the contact page, or a phone call to the nearer store. A photograph of the equipment pad saves a visit more often than not.
  • We book a window. A named day and a two-hour window, not “sometime next week”. Eugene and Bend run separate routes, so ask for the store nearer you.
  • We quote before we fix. The technician diagnoses on site and tells you the cost before doing the work. Parts we stock go on the van; the rest we order and schedule.

Where we come out to

Roughly an hour from each store: Eugene, Springfield, Creswell, Junction City and Cottage Grove from the Eugene shop; Bend, Redmond, Sisters, Sunriver and La Pine from the Bend shop. Further out, ask — we will tell you whether it is worth your money.
