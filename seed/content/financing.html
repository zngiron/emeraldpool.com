Financing

Pay for it over a few winters

We finance through Wells Fargo Retail Services. The application takes about ten minutes in either showroom, and you will have a decision before you leave.

Is 0% interest real?

Yes, on promotional terms and subject to credit approval. The condition worth reading is that interest is deferred rather than waived on some plans: clear the balance inside the promotional window and you pay none, miss it and interest is charged from the purchase date. We will say which plan you are being offered, out loud, before you sign.

Does applying affect my credit score?

The application is a hard credit enquiry, which can move a score by a few points. One enquiry, once. We do not shop your application around several lenders.

Can I finance delivery, the pad and the electrical?

Delivery and any accessories we supply go on the same agreement. Work done by your own electrician or concrete contractor is billed by them and cannot be included.

What happens if I pay it off early?

Nothing unpleasant. There is no prepayment penalty on any plan we offer.
