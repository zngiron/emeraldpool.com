We are a shop, not an advertising business. We collect what we need to quote, deliver and service a spa, and nothing else.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, how to reach you, which store is nearer and what you are trying to do. It goes to one inbox, read by the people who answer the phone.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your details with anyone outside the two stores.
  • We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this site.
  • We do not set a cookie unless you are signed in to the site as staff.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become orders are deleted after twelve months. Order and service records are kept for as long as we hold parts and warranty history for the spa, which is how we can still service a machine we sold in 1996.

Asking for your record

Call (541) 688-1090 or write through the contact page and ask for a copy of what we hold, or for it to be deleted. We will confirm within thirty days.
